Profile format version 1
========================

The installer reads repository-managed profiles from:

  profiles/boards/*.conf
  profiles/toolchange/*.conf

Files are ordered by filename with LC_ALL=C. Metadata uses key=value lines and
ends at a line containing exactly "---". Files must use LF line endings, end
with a newline, and must not contain tabs or other control characters.

Board profiles
--------------

Required scalar fields:

  format_version=1
  name=<menu label>
  max_tools=<integer from 1 to 16>
  config_note=<note written to multihotend.cfg>

Repeatable fields:

  generate_note=<line printed after generation>
  completion_note=<line printed in the final checklist>

The body after "---" is one Klipper aliases block. It must define each TnH and
TnS from T0 through max_tools - 1 exactly once. Extra aliases such as IOn,
FANn, or RGB are allowed.

Toolchange profiles
-------------------

Required scalar fields:

  format_version=1
  name=<menu label>
  description=<short menu description or project URL>
  hardware_mode=prompt|shared_extruder|multi_toolhead
  release_macro=<macro called by multitool_release_tool>
  pickup_macro=<macro called by multitool_pickup_tool>

Repeatable fields:

  completion_note=<line printed in the final checklist>

The body after "---" is the complete change_tool.cfg template. It must define
the declared release and pickup macros exactly once and must not redefine the
public multitool_release_tool or multitool_pickup_tool hooks.

A template may contain one per-tool expansion block:

  # BEGIN MULTITOOL_TOOL_TEMPLATE
  variable_t@@TOOL@@_dock_x: 0
  # END MULTITOOL_TOOL_TEMPLATE

The installer repeats that block for every configured tool and replaces every
@@TOOL@@ token inside the block with its zero-based tool number.
